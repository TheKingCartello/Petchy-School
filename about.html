Welcome to Petchy School

About Petchy School

Discover the story, values, and people behind our community, where every learner is encouraged to grow and reach their potential.

Our History

Petchy Nursery, Primary and Secondary School was established on 9 September 2022 under the leadership of Mr Peter Oluemaka Okafor and Mrs Chioma Okafor. The school operates under a licence granted by the Delta State Ministry of Education.

Since its establishment, Petchy School has remained committed to providing quality education and nurturing responsible children. We support every learner's academic, moral, social, and spiritual development.

Our teachers and staff work in partnership with families to help learners grow into responsible, confident individuals who contribute to their communities with integrity and excellence.

Mission, Vision & Core Values

Our Mission

To provide an engaging, high-quality, holistic education through excellent teaching, innovative technology, and learning experiences tailored to each student.

Our Vision

We aim to develop students who demonstrate resilience, respect, integrity, and independence. We prepare them for the future and encourage them to become confident, happy, and active local and global citizens.

Our Core Values

  • Excellence
  • Integrity
  • Faith
  • Discipline
  • Service
  • Respect
  • Honesty
  • Hard work
  • Kindness
  • Responsibility

A Message from Our Proprietor

Mr Peter Oluemaka OkaforProprietor

It is my great pleasure to welcome you to Petchy Nursery, Primary and Secondary School, a growing institution committed to shaping lives, building character, and preparing young minds for a purposeful future.

At Petchy School, we believe that education goes beyond academic achievement. Our responsibility is to develop the whole child, academically, morally, socially, emotionally, and spiritually. We strive to equip every learner with the knowledge, confidence, discipline, resilience, and values required to make meaningful contributions to society.

Our progress has been made possible through the dedication of our teachers and staff, the partnership of parents, and the continued support of the wider community. Together, we are building a school culture where every child is valued, encouraged to discover their potential, and empowered to pursue excellence.

With warm regards,
Mr Peter Oluemaka Okafor

A Message from Our Proprietress

Mrs Chioma OkaforProprietress

A warm welcome to Petchy Nursery, Primary and Secondary School, a place where every child is welcomed, valued, guided, and encouraged to become the very best version of themselves.

As a school, we understand that entrusting your child to us is a responsibility that goes far beyond the classroom. Parents place in our care not only their children’s education, but also their hopes, dreams, values, and aspirations. We therefore strive to create an atmosphere where children feel safe to learn, confident to express themselves, and inspired to discover their unique abilities.

At Petchy School, we believe that true education is about developing the whole child. Alongside academic excellence, we nurture good manners, discipline, respect, responsibility, compassion, resilience, and sound moral and spiritual values. Our goal is to help our learners grow into individuals who are knowledgeable, confident, considerate, and prepared to make a positive difference wherever they find themselves.

With warm regards,
Mrs Chioma Okafor
