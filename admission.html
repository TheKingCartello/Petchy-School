Admissions at Petchy School

Join our Creche, Nursery, Basic, Junior Secondary or Senior Secondary classes. Visit the administrative office for admission enquiries and registration forms.

Classes Available

  • Creche
  • Pre-Nursery
  • Nursery 1–3
  • Basic 1–6
  • Junior Secondary (JS 1–3)
  • Senior Secondary (SS 1–2)

Admission Process

  1. Get the Registration Form

    Pick up a registration form from the school administrative office.

  2. Placement Test

    Your child sits a written placement test.

  3. Admission Status

    Visit or contact the school office for an update on your child's admission status.

Enquiries and Registration Forms

For all enquiries, complaints and admission matters, please visit the administrative block. You can make enquiries in person and collect a registration form from the office.
