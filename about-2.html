Mission, Vision & Core Values

Our Mission

Building a greater society, by raising generational leaders through qualitive basic education and the word of God that permeates the Spirit, Soul and Body.

Our Vision

To continually improve on step by step teaching techniques, in our pursuit to being among the top 10 schools within 10years in every locality.

Our Core Values

Excellence • Integrity • Faith • Discipline • Service • Leadership

Our History

God’s Heritage International School began with a divine vision to nurture young minds in both academics and faith. Established officially in 2015 after years of planning, the school started as a nursery and primary institution before expanding into a thriving secondary school.

Since inception, the school has become a beacon of excellence in Asaba, Delta State — offering a holistic education that balances intellectual growth, moral strength, and spiritual development.

With dedicated educators, modern facilities, and a faith-driven curriculum, God’s Heritage continues to stand out as a place where students are molded into leaders who influence their world with integrity and excellence.

About the Proprietor

Pastor Frederick Okoka is the visionary founder and proprietor of God’s Heritage International School. His passion for education began in 2000 at Hollywood International School, Asaba, where he served diligently for ten years, rising from classroom teacher to School Administrator.

In 2010, he moved to Lumen Christi Private School as Principal, where his leadership transformed the school’s population and reputation within a short time. Inspired by his calling, Pastor Okoka founded God’s Heritage International School to combine academic rigor with Christian principles.

Under his guidance, the school has grown into a respected institution producing students of excellence, character, and godly influence.
